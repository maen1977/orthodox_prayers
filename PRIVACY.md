# Privacy Policy — Church Prayers

**Application:** Church Prayers / Orthodox Agenda and Prayers  
**Provider:** Maen Hanouna Satellite  
**Contact phone:** +962 78 827 2988  
**Last updated:** 2 August 2026

The application does not require an account, does not display advertisements, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs in this release.

Internet access is used to download signed daily liturgical data and a short source-comparison report over HTTPS. Ordinary hosting logs, such as an IP address and request time, may be processed by the hosting provider under its own policy.

Preferences, favorites, reading position, search history, notes, and verified data copies remain on the device. The application does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or personal files. Notification permission is optional and is used only for reminders selected by the user.

The project does not maintain user accounts or account data. Local application data can be removed by clearing storage or uninstalling the application.

The public web version of this policy is maintained in `docs/privacy/index.html`.
